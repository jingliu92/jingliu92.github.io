# 02-03 What is a company worth?

## 1. Overview

A company's value is not simply the value of its physical assets. Its ability to generate future cash flows is also important.

## 2. What is company worth?

A company that generates $1 every year forever is not worth infinity. Its value today depends on how much we discount future cash flows.

- Lower interest rate → Higher company value.
- Higher interest rate → Lower company value.

This is the foundation of the Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation method.

## 3. Why company value matters?

The goal of investing is to buy stocks when their market prices are below their true value and sell when prices are above their true value.

### 3.1. True Value vs. Market Price

- True value (intrinsic value): An estimate of what a company is actually worth based on its underlying fundamentals.
- Market price: The price investors are currently willing to pay for the stock.

Market prices fluctuate, while the company's underlying value may change more gradually.
