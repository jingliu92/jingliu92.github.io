## 2. Grinold's Fundamental Law

<img width="1156" height="414" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8ff1f6b3-fe80-4b92-854f-4a6e9c137129" />

[Portfolio Managment Research](https://www.pm-research.com/richard-grinold)

In the 1980s, Richard Grinold was seeking a method of relating performance, skill, and breadth in investing. He then developed a relationship he called the `Fundamental Law of Active Portfolio Management` where we could **combine skill and breadth to create an estimate of performance**.

<img width="730" height="449" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d68a4c10-5768-460f-a2dd-ec7439a8e501" />

`The Fundamental Law of Active Portfolio Management`

- Performance = skill *  sqrt(breadth) or IR = IC * sqrt(BR)
  
Thus Two ways to improve performance:

1) improve skill;
   
2) more opportunities for applying that skill

- Breadth might relate to how many stocks you invest in, it is the number of trading opportunities.
- Skill relates to your skill in choosing them, is summarized in Information coefficient
- Performance is summarized by information ratio (IR)

## 3. The coin flipping casino: Understanding Risk and Diversification

This thought experiment connects betting strategies with stock investing, particularly the relationship between expected returns and risk.

<img width="951" height="552" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d17772ab-900a-480a-9a86-8dde2377db44" />

### 3.1. The Setup
- There are 1,000 betting tables, each with a biased coin.
- Probability of Heads (winning): 51%.
- Probability of Tails (losing): 49%.
- You have 1,000 tokens to invest.
- You can distribute your tokens across the tables however you like.

### 3.2. Betting Rules

This is an even-money bet:
<img width="632" height="116" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3c76a6ef-8329-449a-a8a9-172a3220a0fe" />

For example, betting 10 tokens:
- Win: Gain 10 tokens.
- Lose: Lose 10 tokens.

### 3.3 The Key Question: How Should You Allocate Your Tokens?

Two possible strategies:

Strategy A: Concentrated Betting
- Bet all 1,000 tokens on one coin flip.
- Potential outcome: +1,000 or -1,000 tokens.
- Very high risk.

Strategy B: Diversified Betting
- Distribute tokens across many coin flips.
- Individual outcomes vary, but gains and losses can offset each other.
- Lower overall risk.
